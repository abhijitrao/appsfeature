Privacy Policy.

A straightforward explanation of how the AppsFeature website handles information.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the public website at appsfeature.com (the “Website”), operated under the AppsFeature name by its independent developer. It describes the Website only. Individual applications or tools listed here may have their own privacy notices and practices.

1. Information you provide

The Website does not currently provide user accounts, contact forms, comments or newsletter subscriptions. If you contact us using the email link, your email application will send the information you choose to include. We receive and use that information to respond to your message.

2. Website hosting information

The Website is a static site hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic or security logs. We do not operate a separate visitor database or directly control the hosting provider’s infrastructure logs. Please refer to GitHub’s privacy statement for details about its processing.

3. Cookies and analytics

The Website does not intentionally set advertising cookies, run third-party analytics or use browser local storage for visitor tracking. Your browser or hosting provider may handle essential technical data as part of delivering and securing the Website.

4. Third-party services and links

The Website links to third-party services, including GitHub, and loads typography from Google Fonts. When your browser accesses those services, the relevant provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control third-party websites or services.

5. Applications listed on AppsFeature

Applications and tools linked from this Website may process information on your device or through their own services. Their data practices depend on each product and its features. Review the privacy notice supplied with an application before using it. This Website policy is not the privacy policy for DroidShare WiFi, Network Simulator or any other software product.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable care when handling messages sent directly to us. Email communications may remain in the mail systems used by you and us for as long as needed to respond, maintain business records or meet legal obligations. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

This Website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not send personal information about a child unless you are authorised to do so.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to contact us or follow external links. For a question or request about information you have sent directly to AppsFeature, email appsfeature@gmail.com. Rights available to you may depend on the laws that apply to your location.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Website or its services change. The latest version will be published here with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact appsfeature@gmail.com.
